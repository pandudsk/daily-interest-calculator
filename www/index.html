Reports & Printing

Print statements, both separate day-wise ledgers and transaction history using Android Print Service. Bluetooth thermal printers may need a compatible ESC/POS print service or manufacturer app. USB OTG requires a compatible Android driver/app.

Wi-Fi printers appear through Android Print Service when installed and connected. Direct Bluetooth / USB printing is model- and driver-dependent; this app does not claim universal device compatibility.

Interest settings
